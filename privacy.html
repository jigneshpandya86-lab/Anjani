How Anjani Water handles information you share when requesting a quote, order or sample.

When you submit a form or contact us, we may receive your name, phone number, email address, delivery area, address, product requirement, quantity and preferred contact time.

How we use it

We use these details to respond to your enquiry, prepare a quote, check delivery availability, process an accepted order and provide customer support. We do not sell your contact information.

Sharing and retention

Your request may be stored in our enquiry system and shared only with staff or service providers needed to respond to it. We retain it only as long as reasonably needed for sales, support and record-keeping.
